Summary

This scanner is designed for professional security assessments. We collect minimal, privacy-first analytics to improve reliability, capacity planning, and security. We do not collect or sell personal data. We do not use cross‑site tracking or fingerprinting.

Data We Collect (Analytics)

  • Aggregated scan activity (e.g., scan_started, scan_completed, scan_failed).
  • Anonymous user identifiers derived from API keys or client IDs (non‑reversible).
  • Truncated or hashed IP addresses for abuse prevention and rate limiting.
  • Aggregated vulnerability counts by severity and type.
  • Hashed target domain identifiers (no raw URLs stored).

We do not store raw URLs, payloads, or PII in analytics logs.
